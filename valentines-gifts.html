Valentine's gift questions

Is there a matching goth outfit for couples?

Yes. The Night Session Rash Guard comes in a women's and a men's cut with the same thorn and wave pattern, and the Night Session One Piece shares the same print. Two Wordmark Tees also make a matching pair and come to $76, which ships free in the US.

Can I ship a gift straight to someone else?

Yes. Enter their name and address as the shipping address at checkout and the order goes straight to them. Your receipt and tracking come to your email.

What if the size is wrong?

Pieces are printed to order, so we cannot take returns for the wrong size. Every product page has a size chart with exact measurements. Misprinted, damaged or defective pieces are replaced free within 30 days of delivery.

Can I buy Valentine Tide on Etsy instead?

Yes. Every piece in this guide is also in the Valentine Tide Etsy shop at valentinetide.etsy.com, printed to order the same way. Etsy prices include shipping, so they read a little higher than on valentinetide.com.
