Shipping

Every piece is printed to order, then shipped. Most orders leave within 5 to 10 business days, and a tracking link is emailed as soon as yours is on its way.

Where
United States
Cost
$5 flat, free on orders over $75
Time
5 to 10 business days to ship, plus transit

International shipping is on the way. Join the list on the home page to hear when it opens.

Returns and replacements

Because each piece is printed just for you, we cannot take returns for change of mind or the wrong size, so please check the fit notes below before you order.

If something arrives misprinted, damaged, or defective, we will make it right. Email us within 30 days of delivery with your order number and a photo, and we will send a free replacement. No need to send the original back.

Sizing and fit

Wordmark Tee

Sizes S to 3XL

Relaxed, boxy fit on a heavyweight garment dyed blank. Between sizes? Size down for a closer cut.

Heartbreak Crop Tee

Sizes S to 2XL

Fitted and cropped to sit at a high waist. Size up for a looser fit.

Tip: lay a tee you already love flat and measure across the chest, armpit to armpit. Compare that with the fit notes above, and if you are unsure, email us and we will help you pick.

Care

  • Turn it inside out and wash cold with darks.
  • Hang dry, or tumble dry on low.
  • Never iron directly on the print.
  • Rinse out salt water after a session so the black stays deep.

Privacy

Payments run through Stripe Checkout, so we never see or store your card number. We use your address only to print and ship your order. If you join the list, we use your email to announce drops and nothing else. We never sell your data, and you can unsubscribe anytime.
