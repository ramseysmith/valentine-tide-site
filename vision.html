Our vision

Surf the Shadows

Surf culture sold us one picture: blond, sunburnt, smiling at noon. We paddle out for a different reason.

Manifesto

The ocean was never pastel. It is cold, deep, and older than any of us. We dress for that ocean.

Valentine Tide was born where the last light drowns and the water turns to ink. We make apparel for the ones who paddle out under a dead moon, who wear black to the beach without apology, and who find their calm somewhere past the break, in the cold and the quiet and the dark.

Goth and surf are not opposites. Both are about devotion to something bigger and stranger than you. Both have their rituals, their uniforms, their hours when everyone else has gone home. Valentine Tide lives in the space where they meet.

What We Stand On

Night Water

Our palette is the sea after sunset: near black, bone, and one red that runs like the tide. Every piece is designed to look right in low light, on wet sand, under a streetlamp on the drive home.

Bleeding Heart

Valentine means devotion. Our graphics are romantic and a little broken: skulls in hearts, thorns in the swell. Made for us, never pulled from a stock library.

Heavy Threads

We choose heavyweight, garment dyed blanks that soften and fade like salt worn rubber. Pieces made to be lived in for years, not tossed after a season.

Who it is for

Made for the ones who paddle out in black

The dawn patrol regular with chipped black nail polish. The kid who learned to surf between shows. The one who brings a thermos of black coffee and stays until the moon is up. If you have ever felt out of place in a surf shop, this is your shop.

The world

Black Sand, Black Boards

Red filigree, skulls, and crossbones. Boards racked on volcanic rock while the swell comes in grey. This is the coast we design for: moody, rugged, and a little dangerous.

Every graphic we print draws on the same world, so what you wear looks like it belongs in the water with you.
